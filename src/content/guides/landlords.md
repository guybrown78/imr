If you supply an electrical appliance with a let, it's your responsibility to make sure it's safe, both at the start of the tenancy and while the tenant is using it.

## Do landlords legally need PAT testing?
For a standard single let in England, PAT testing isn't specifically required. Your EICR covers the fixed wiring. But:

- **HMO licences** often require PAT records as a condition.
- **Letting agents** usually ask for a certificate before marketing the property.
- **Insurers** may want evidence that appliances were checked.

## Which items?
Only the appliances **you** supply, such as the kettle, toaster, microwave, washing machine, fridge freezer, vacuum, lamps and any heaters. Tenants' own items aren't your responsibility.

## How often?
We recommend testing at **every change of tenancy**, and **every year for HMOs**.
