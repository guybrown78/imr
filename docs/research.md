## What successful PAT testing sites elsewhere do
Sources looked at: Housekeep, Fantastic Services, Direct365, A-OK Group and UK PAT Ltd.
1. Prices above the fold, with the minimum charge and VAT made explicit.
2. An instant quote driven by one input: the number of appliances.
3. Phone and quote CTAs always visible, with a CTA repeated after every section.
4. Trust near the top: reviews, City & Guilds 2377, insurance and years trading.
5. Sector framing: offices, landlords, schools, care, construction (110V) and events.
6. One page per town with local content.
7. A dated cost guide ("[Month Year]") plus FAQ content to capture "People also ask".
8. A-OK: a short punchy hero, an accreditation strip, a review widget and a plain friendly tone.
